# Notifications

Notifications tell you when something needs you: a mention, a direct message, a task assigned to you, a request waiting for your decision. They collect in the bell at the top of the app. This page needs the `notify` module, which is on by default.

## The bell

The bell shows how many notifications you have not read. Open it to see the list, select a notification to go to what it is about, or choose **Mark all as read**.

Notifications arrive live while the app is open. When you are offline, they wait in the list.

## What you are told about

| Area | Notifications |
| --- | --- |
| Chat | Mentions of you, direct messages, and replies in threads you follow |
| Tasks | Tasks assigned to you, tasks due soon, and overdue tasks |
| Helpdesk | New tickets, tickets assigned to you, ticket status changes, and comments on tickets |
| Approvals | Approval requests sent to you, and approved, rejected or cancelled requests you sent |

## Settings

Open **Notification settings** from the account menu. It has three parts.

**Notification types.** Each type has a switch. A type that is off is not sent to you. Use this to turn off, for example, overdue reminders you do not want.

**Muted channels.** A muted channel sends you no notifications, even when someone mentions you there. To mute a channel, open its options in Messenger and choose **Mute channel**. Unmute it from the same place, or from the list under **Muted channels** in the settings.

**Do not disturb.** Pick a **From** and an **Until** time. Set both, or clear both. The start and the end cannot be the same time. Between those times no pop-up appears. Notifications are still kept and counted in the bell. The hours follow your time zone from your profile.

## How long notifications last

Read notifications are deleted after about 90 days. Unread ones are kept until you read them.

## Not in v0.1.0

Push notifications and email notifications are planned. In v0.1.0, notifications reach you only in the app. Sign-up and password emails are a different matter: those are sent to your email address. See [Account](/guide/account).
