# Account

Your account is your identity across every workspace you belong to. This page covers signing up, signing in, verifying your email, resetting a password, your profile, and the devices you are signed in on.

## Sign up

Open the sign-up page and enter:

- your **display name**, which other people see
- your **email address**, which must not already have an account
- a **password** of 8 to 128 characters

Samnak Chat then sends a verification email to that address. Open the link in it to verify your email.

You do not need to verify your email before you use the app. Until you do, a banner asks you to verify. You can ask for the email again from the banner. A verification link is valid for one hour.

Signing up does not join you to a workspace. Use an invite link from a workspace owner or admin, or create your own workspace. See [Workspace](/guide/workspace).

## Sign in

Enter your email and password. A wrong email or password gives the same message for both, so nobody can tell which one was wrong.

Five failed sign-ins in one minute, for one email address or from one IP address, are refused for a short time. The page then says **Too many attempts. Wait a moment and try again.**

## Forgot your password

Choose **Forgot password?** and enter your email. If an account uses that address, Samnak Chat sends a link to set a new password. The page gives the same answer whether or not the account exists. The link is valid for one hour.

After you set a new password, every device is signed out. Sign in again with the new password.

## Profile

Open **Profile** from the account menu. You can change:

- your **display name**
- your **preferred language**, Thai or English. The interface changes to that language.
- your **time zone**, such as `Asia/Bangkok`. Times in the app, and the do-not-disturb hours in [notifications](/guide/notifications), use this zone.

Times are stored in UTC and shown in your time zone.

## Theme and language

The toggle at the top of the app switches between light and dark. Until you pick one, the app follows your computer's setting. Your choice is remembered on that device.

The language picker is in the top bar on larger screens, and in the account menu on phones. Its change lasts until you reload the page. To keep a language, set **Preferred language** in Profile. The app then opens in that language on every device.

## Devices

Open **Devices** to see every device that is signed in to your account, with when each was last used. The device you are on is marked **This device**.

- **Revoke** a device to sign it out. Its session ends at once.
- **Sign out of all devices** ends every session, including this one.

Samnak Chat stays signed in on a device for up to 30 days of inactivity. If someone reuses an old session token from a device, that device is signed out.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** from the account menu. This signs out only this device.
